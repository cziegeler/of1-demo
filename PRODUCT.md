# LEGO.com — OF1 Demo Product Brief

## Brand Overview

LEGO is the world's leading construction-toy brand, known for interlocking plastic bricks and themed building sets. LEGO.com is the flagship direct-to-consumer e-commerce destination — a product catalog, community hub, and content platform in one. The site blends editorial storytelling (designer videos, building tips) with a full shopping experience (product detail pages, wishlists, loyalty rewards via LEGO Insiders).

## Demo Focus

**LEGO Technic — Supercars & Motorsport Collection**

The demo showcases OF1's personalized content generation on LEGO's highest-AOV product line: Technic supercars. These are 18+ sets priced $149–$599 that target adult fans of LEGO (AFOLs) who are passionate about automotive engineering, motorsport, and display-worthy builds.

## Brand Voice & Tone

- **Playful precision**: technical accuracy wrapped in approachable language
- **Builder-first**: always frames the experience as a journey ("build", "create", "discover")
- **Confident, never aggressive**: CTAs are invitations, not commands ("Add to Bag", "Explore the build")
- **Premium for Technic/18+**: slightly elevated register — "engineered", "authentic", "masterpiece"
- **Inclusive joy**: building is for everyone, all ages, all skill levels

## Visual Identity

- **Primary background**: White (#FFFFFF)
- **Brand yellow**: #FFD500 (accents, highlights, loyalty badges)
- **Text**: Black (#000000) primary, #333 secondary
- **CTA red**: #FD0000 (Add to Bag), occasionally blue for secondary actions
- **Typography**: Cera Pro (headings), system sans-serif fallback (body)
- **Imagery**: Product photography on white/lifestyle with depth-of-field; sets shown built at 3/4 angle
- **Card style**: White card, subtle shadow, rounded corners (8px), product image top 60%

## Anti-References (What NOT to Do)

- Do NOT use childish language or imagery — the Technic audience is adult
- Do NOT show minifigures — Technic supercars don't include them
- Do NOT use generic stock photography — only real LEGO product images
- Do NOT write discounts or fake urgency ("ONLY 3 LEFT!") — LEGO doesn't do that
- Do NOT use Comic Sans, Impact, or novelty fonts
- Do NOT place text over busy product images — use white space
- Do NOT mention competitors by name
- Do NOT invent set numbers or piece counts — use real catalog data only

## Strategic Principles for the Demo

1. **Authenticity**: Every product name, price, piece count, and image must be real LEGO catalog data.
2. **Personalization angle**: Show how OF1 adapts hero content, product ordering, and CTAs for Marcus's motorsport interest vs. generic browsing.
3. **Premium feel**: The Technic line is LEGO's aspirational tier. The demo should feel like a luxury automotive configurator, not a toy store.
4. **Engineering narrative**: Highlight functional elements (working gearbox, opening doors, steering) that differentiate Technic from standard sets.
5. **Conversion clarity**: Every section should have a clear path-to-purchase; no dead ends.
6. **Loyalty integration**: Reference LEGO Insiders points and VIP perks naturally.
