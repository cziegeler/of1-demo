Proposed Demo

Demo Focus: LEGO Technic — Supercars & Motorsport

The Technic line represents LEGO's most visually stunning and technically impressive product category for adults. With the McLaren P1 ($449), Ferrari Daytona SP3 ($449), Red Bull F1 car ($229), and Fast & Furious collaborations, this line combines brand partnerships, engineering detail, and premium pricing that makes for a compelling demo narrative.

Demo Narrative

Persona: "Marcus" — a 32-year-old motorsport enthusiast and adult LEGO fan (AFOL). Works in tech, follows F1, has disposable income. Browsing for his next weekend project — a premium display piece that combines his love of engineering and supercars.

Journey: Marcus lands on the LEGO homepage → navigates to Technic → browses the supercar collection → dives into the McLaren P1 product page with its rich feature storytelling, 360° views, and engineering quotes → adds to bag. Along the way, personalized CTAs nudge him toward LEGO Insiders for points and exclusive early access.

Rationale

  • Visual impact — Supercars photograph beautifully; product imagery is dramatic and aspirational
  • Rich product pages — McLaren P1 has features carousel, designer quotes, owner's club, 15-image gallery
  • Brand partnerships — McLaren, Ferrari, Red Bull, BMW, Fast & Furious — instant recognition
  • Clear conversion path — Price → Add to Bag → Insiders points → cross-sell carousel
  • Adult audience — Premium pricing ($65-$449) signals quality; "18+" badge creates exclusivity
  • Current momentum — F1 and Fast & Furious are culturally hot; new releases driving traffic

Design System Observations

  • Typography — Clean sans-serif (Cera Pro), bold headings, compact product text
  • Colors — White background, LEGO yellow accents (#FFD500), black text, red for badges/CTAs
  • Cards — Minimal borders, generous whitespace, hover states reveal secondary images

  • Icons — Custom SVG for age/pieces/rating metrics
